# Step 03 – Motif vocabulary: 15 motifs

Cut from the 17 candidates: **Rememory** (only two books) and **Double
consciousness** (on this shelf it selected almost the same books as
intersectionality; it earns its place back if the catalogue grows).

Each motif has two Polish registers: the **shop name**, which is what a reader
sees on a product page, and the **scholarly term**, which belongs in the
definition. Otherwise the page ends up saying *chrononormatywność*.

Polish renderings are marked **[ustalone]** where the term already circulates in
Polish criticism in that form, and **[propozycja]** where it is mine. Say the word
and I will verify any of the first kind against a source.

Confidence on assignments – **firm** (the book is a standard example),
**fair** (defensible, not canonical).

---

## 01 · The madwoman in the attic
**Gniew nazwany chorobą** · termin: wariatka na strychu **[ustalone]**
*Sandra Gilbert & Susan Gubar, "The Madwoman in the Attic", 1979*

The woman shut away and called ill, standing as the double of the obedient
heroine – the anger the good woman is not allowed to carry.

- Jane Eyre – **firm** (the term was coined on this novel)
- Wide Sargasso Sea – **firm** (Rhys wrote the madwoman's side of it)
- The Bell Jar – **firm**
- Nightwood – **fair**
- Her Body and Other Parties – **fair**

## 02 · Compulsory heterosexuality
**Heteroseksualność z obowiązku** · termin: przymusowa heteroseksualność **[ustalone]**
*Adrienne Rich, "Compulsory Heterosexuality and Lesbian Existence", 1980*

Heterosexuality as an institution that is enforced and maintained, rather than a
preference anyone was asked about.

- Stone Butch Blues – **firm**
- Nightwood – **firm**
- The Handmaid's Tale – **firm**

## 03 · The mother–daughter plot
**Matka i córka** · termin: fabuła matki i córki **[propozycja]**
*Marianne Hirsch, "The Mother/Daughter Plot", 1989*

The story told from the daughter's side and then from the mother's, where what is
inherited is not property but a way of surviving.

- Beloved – **firm**
- The Color Purple – **firm**
- Kim Jiyoung, Born 1982 – **firm**

## 04 · Motherhood as institution
**Macierzyństwo jako instytucja** · **[ustalone]**
*Adrienne Rich, "Of Woman Born", 1976 – po polsku "Zrodzone z kobiety"*

What is done with the reproductive body once someone else decides what it is for.

- The Handmaid's Tale – **firm**
- Beloved – **firm**
- Kim Jiyoung, Born 1982 – **firm**
- Detransition, Baby – **firm**

## 05 · The female Bildungsroman
**Dorastanie dziewczyny** · termin: kobiecy Bildungsroman **[ustalone]**
*Elizabeth Abel, Marianne Hirsch & Elizabeth Langland, "The Voyage In", 1983*

Growing up as a woman, where the plot is not the acquisition of a place in the
world but the discovery that the place is smaller than promised.

- Jane Eyre – **firm**
- My Brilliant Friend – **firm**
- Americanah – **fair**
- Girl, Woman, Other – **fair**
- Middlesex – **fair**

## 06 · The woman as artist
**Własny pokój** · **[ustalone]**
*Virginia Woolf, "A Room of One's Own", 1929 – po polsku "Własny pokój"*

What it costs a woman to make the work, and what has to be arranged before she can.

- The Bell Jar – **firm**
- My Brilliant Friend – **firm**
- Orlando – **fair**

> The one place where the Polish name is better than the English. *Własny pokój*
> is instantly recognisable here and says the whole thing in two words.

## 07 · Passing
**Uchodzić za kogoś innego** · termin: passing **[propozycja – polska krytyka
zwykle zostawia angielskie słowo]**
*From Nella Larsen's "Passing", 1929; established as a critical category by
Elaine Ginsberg, "Passing and the Fictions of Identity", 1996*

Being read as something one is not, and the cost of both the reading and the
correction.

- Middlesex – **firm**
- Orlando – **firm**
- Detransition, Baby – **firm**
- Stone Butch Blues – **firm**
- Americanah – **fair** (the hair, the accent, the American self)
- Convenience Store Woman – **fair** (Keiko assembles a normal self out of her
  colleagues' speech and clothes)

## 08 · Intersectionality
**Nie da się przeżyć osobno** · termin: intersekcjonalność **[ustalone]**
*Kimberlé Crenshaw, 1989 – though Davis's book of 1981 is one of the works that
made the term necessary*

Race, class and gender as one situation rather than three, because they are not
survivable separately.

- Women, Race & Class – **firm**
- Girl, Woman, Other – **firm**
- Americanah – **fair**

## 09 · The angel in the house
**Anioł w domu** · **[ustalone]**
*Coventry Patmore's poem, 1854, turned into a critical term by Virginia Woolf in
"Professions for Women", 1931, where she describes having to kill her*

The self-effacing feminine ideal – the woman who wants nothing – and what happens
to whoever tries to be her.

- Kim Jiyoung, Born 1982 – **firm**
- The Bell Jar – **firm**
- Convenience Store Woman – **firm**
- Jane Eyre – **fair**
- The Color Purple – **fair**

## 10 · Who holds the gaze
**Kto patrzy** · termin: męskie spojrzenie **[ustalone, ale węższe]**
*Laura Mulvey, "Visual Pleasure and Narrative Cinema", 1975*

Who is looking, who is looked at, and what changes when that turns round.

- The Power – **firm** (the reversal is the premise)
- The Handmaid's Tale – **firm**
- Her Body and Other Parties – **fair**

> The Polish term names only half of it. *Męskie spojrzenie* is the established
> rendering, but The Power is here precisely because the gaze changes hands, so
> the shop name has to stay wider than the scholarly one.

## 11 · Female friendship as a plot
**Przyjaźń jako fabuła** · **[propozycja]**
*Sharon Marcus, "Between Women", 2007 – friendship between women as a structure
that carries a novel, not as decoration around a marriage plot*

- My Brilliant Friend – **firm**
- Girl, Woman, Other – **firm**
- The Color Purple – **fair**

## 12 · Gender as performance
**Płeć jest robiona** · termin: performatywność płci **[ustalone]**
*Judith Butler, "Gender Trouble", 1990 – po polsku "Uwikłani w płeć"*

Gender as something done repeatedly rather than something possessed.

- Orlando – **firm**
- Middlesex – **firm**
- Stone Butch Blues – **firm**
- Detransition, Baby – **firm**

## 13 · The subaltern who cannot speak
**Kto dostaje głos** · termin: podporządkowana inna **[ustalone]**
*Gayatri Chakravorty Spivak, "Three Women's Texts and a Critique of Imperialism",
1985, and "Can the Subaltern Speak?", 1988 – the first is written partly about
Jane Eyre and Wide Sargasso Sea*

Who is given a voice in the story, who is spoken about, and who is used as the
material the heroine grows on.

- Wide Sargasso Sea – **firm**
- Beloved – **fair**
- Women, Race & Class – **fair**

## 14 · The monstrous feminine
**Kobieta jako potwór** · termin: kobieca potworność **[propozycja]**
*Barbara Creed, "The Monstrous-Feminine", 1993*

The woman made into a horror, and the question of whose fear she is made of.

- Her Body and Other Parties – **firm**
- The Power – **fair**
- Nightwood – **fair**

## 15 · The timetable a life is supposed to keep
**Życie o czasie** · termin: chrononormatywność **[ustalone]**
*Elizabeth Freeman, "Time Binds", 2010 – chrononormativity, defined as the
organisation of human bodies toward maximum productivity*

School, then work, then marriage, then a child, each at the hour society expects
it – and the judgement that falls on a woman who is off the schedule rather than
doing anything wrong.

- Convenience Store Woman – **firm**
- Kim Jiyoung, Born 1982 – **firm**
- Detransition, Baby – **firm**
- The Bell Jar – **fair** (the fig tree is an image of exactly this)
- My Brilliant Friend – **fair** (Lila married at sixteen)

> Overlaps with **the angel in the house** on two books. The angel says what a
> woman should *be*; this one says *when* she should have done what.

## Coverage

Every book carries at least two motifs; sixteen of nineteen carry three or more.
The three on two are **The Power**, **Wide Sargasso Sea** and
**Women, Race & Class** – books whose weight sits in one place.
